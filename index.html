From discovery to a useful shortlist

How to use a HooBuy spreadsheet

A HooBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize items someone may wish to source through a shopping agent. A useful row preserves the original listing, the exact variant and the questions still unanswered. This page adds a CAFANS selection and a comparison workflow; it does not confirm that these items can currently be ordered through HooBuy.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the exact item

Browse the CAFANS catalog and locate the product name or reference number. Save its original marketplace listing where available. A product title alone may describe several different versions.

02 / COMPARE

Check size and evidence

Record the chosen color, size, measurements and seller-stated materials. Separate listing photographs from any inspection photos supplied for your actual order.

03 / CONFIRM

Check the buying route

Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the original listing and selected item. Review the current quote, charges, destination restrictions and return terms before payment.

Look closer

QC photos: useful checks before shipping

Quality-control photos can document visible features of an item. Compare the selected color, quantity, size labels, stitching, surface condition and any requested measurements against your order.

For clothing, compare equivalent garment measurements rather than relying only on S, M or L. For shoes, clarify which sizing system the listing uses and whether a supplied length describes the foot, insole or outsole.

If an important detail is unclear, request a closer photograph or measurement and keep the answer beside the product row. Photos cannot confirm every material claim, long-term durability or authenticity.

More than the item price

Compare the complete parcel, too.

A low listing price is only one part of a shopping budget. Ask for a destination-specific shipping estimate and note its weight and dimension assumptions. Packaging changes can affect the quote, and a carrier may use volumetric weight. Use the actual rate and divisor stated for your selected service.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official HooBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent discovery and research guide. The catalog references come from CAFANS, and all shopping buttons open CAFANS. No affiliation with HooBuy is claimed.

Can I order every listed product through HooBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Find the original marketplace listing and confirm that HooBuy currently supports that listing, item type and destination before placing an order.

Are these prices live or all-inclusive?

No. They are reference USD estimates observed on CAFANS. Check the current variant price, exchange rate, fees, delivery charges and applicable taxes before paying.

Can I download the spreadsheet?

With JavaScript enabled, use “Export visible rows” above the catalog. The CSV includes the displayed product references and blank fields for size, color, listing checks and your notes. It can be opened in a spreadsheet application.

What should I do if a saved product link changes?

Search CAFANS using the product name or reference number. Recheck the seller and exact option. Mark an unavailable item clearly in your own sheet instead of assuming a similar replacement is identical.

Do QC photos guarantee quality or authenticity?

No. Photos help inspect visible details within the limits of their lighting, angle and resolution. Keep unsupported claims separate from confirmed measurements and seek appropriate verification when authenticity is important.

Where does “Buy it now” take me?

It opens the CAFANS homepage so you can explore its catalog. This page does not accept payment, reserve stock or create an order.
